Orange Company Brain: one brain for the whole company, where your clearance decides what you find and what you can do.

Built in one afternoon at the Company Brain Hackathon (TechEquity AI, Snowflake Silicon Valley AI Hub, Oct 12 2026), on HydraDB.

Every person at a fake company, Orange, has an AI agent. The agent searches one shared brain as its owner, so HydraDB only returns what that person is cleared for. The CEO sees everything. An engineer sees their projects. A contractor sees one project but not the employee benefits. A reporter outside the company sees only the public website. If you aren't cleared for something, the answer is simply "I don't know."

Agents can act too: file bugs, rerun builds, request time off. The same rules gate every action, enforced by the server, not the AI. A model can't un-know a salary, so we control what it is allowed to fetch.
